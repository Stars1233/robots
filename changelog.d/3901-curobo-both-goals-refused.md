### Fixed: cuRobo refuses a pose goal passed beside a joint goal. Calling `CuroboPolicy.get_actions` with both `target_pose=` and `target_joints=` used to plan the Cartesian goal and drop the joint goal without a word, even though the docs say to pass exactly one and the MoveIt2 provider picks the other way round. Passing both now raises a `ValueError` naming the two keys before anything is planned, so a caller who meant one goal is told which two were seen rather than watching the robot follow the one it did not ask for.
